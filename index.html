Transport from Ascott IFC从雅诗阁IFC出发的交通

Safari Park长隆野生动物世界

DiDi MPV is easiest. Metro alternative: Line 3 from Zhujiang New Town to Hanxi Changlong, then follow resort shuttle / exit instructions.滴滴六座最方便。地铁可从珠江新城乘3号线到汉溪长隆，再按度假区指示转乘接驳车。

Yongqingfang永庆坊

Important planning notes重要旅行提醒

December weather十二月天气

Typical climate normals are about 21°C daytime high and 12°C overnight low. Pack layers, a warmer jacket for evenings and a light stroller blanket.十二月气候常年平均约日间最高21°C、夜间最低12°C。建议分层穿衣，晚上带保暖外套，也可准备婴儿车小毯子。

Stroller + toddler婴儿车与幼儿

Bring a compact stroller, snacks, spare clothes and a portable nap option. Ask car providers in advance about a child seat; do not assume one will be supplied.建议带轻便婴儿车、零食、替换衣物及午睡用品。提前询问司机是否有儿童安全座椅，不要假设车辆一定提供。

Tickets / heights门票与身高

Based on the heights you provided: 122cm (6-year-old) falls in the published 1.0–1.5m child-ticket band; 90cm (2-year-old) is below 1m and may qualify for free admission, subject to one free child per adult. Grandma (66) falls in the published 65+ concession band. Recheck the rules for your exact visit date and bring ID / proof of age. These categories are also commonly used by Safari Park, but confirm its own rules before purchase.根据你提供的身高：6岁孩子122厘米，属于公开票种中的1.0–1.5米儿童优惠票范围；2岁孩子90厘米，低于1米，可能符合免票条件（每位成人限带一名免票儿童）；奶奶66岁，属于公开票种中的65岁以上长者优惠范围。请在购票前按实际游玩日期再次核实，并携带年龄证明／身份证件。长隆野生动物世界也常见类似票种，但须以该园区自己的规则为准。
